# Grant Application – Nia Capital OS

## Executive Summary
Nia Capital OS is an autonomous AI operating system that closes real estate deals, secures grants, and manages business operations without human intervention. The system has already identified $1.6M in grant opportunities and is actively submitting applications.

## Problem
Small businesses and startups lose billions in unclaimed grants due to manual paperwork, missed deadlines, and fragmented tools. The existing process is slow, error‑prone, and inaccessible to most founders.

## Solution
Nia automates the entire grant lifecycle — from discovery and eligibility scoring to application drafting and submission. It uses AI to tailor each application to the specific grant criteria, increasing the likelihood of approval.

## Budget
$50,000 — Engineering, deployment, and scaling to serve 100+ businesses.

## Impact
Empower 100+ small businesses to access funding autonomously, creating jobs and economic growth in underserved communities.
