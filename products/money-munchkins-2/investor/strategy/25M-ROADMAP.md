# $25M Strategic Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Proof
- Public pilot
- 100+ real learners
- Parent feedback
- Learning measurement
- Retention measurement
- First paying customers

## Phase 2 — Product-Market Fit
- 1,000+ learners
- Repeatable acquisition
- Family subscription
- School/program pilot
- Institutional LOIs
- Measurable learning outcomes

## Phase 3 — Platform
- Adaptive learning
- Educator dashboard
- Partner APIs
- Content licensing
- Enterprise analytics
- Physical/digital collectible ecosystem

## Phase 4 — Scale
- 10,000+ learners
- Institutional revenue
- Strategic partnerships
- Licensing revenue
- National distribution

## $25M Valuation Evidence

Target evidence must include:
- Revenue
- Growth
- Retention
- Learning outcomes
- Customer pipeline
- Institutional contracts
- Proprietary IP
- Technology differentiation
- Defensible distribution
