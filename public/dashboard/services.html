Questions

What if the grant doesn't get awarded?

You pay for the draft, not the outcome. We deliver a submission-ready application; the funder makes the decision. If you don't submit it, we don't charge.

Do you submit the application for me?

No. Only you can sign and submit — that's an architectural choice, not a limitation. Every submission goes through your account.

How fast do I get a draft?

Same day. Our system generates the full 10-section application in under two minutes; you review, fill any blanks, and submit the same week.

What kinds of grants do you cover?

Federal (NSF SBIR/STTR, DOE, NIH), state (VIPC, GO Virginia), foundation, corporate, and university programs. Small business, research, education, and community development lanes.

Do you handle minority-owned or women-owned business grants?

Yes. Qualification engine filters specifically for SBA 8(a), WOSB, HUBZone, and Virginia DSBSD-certified programs where applicable.
